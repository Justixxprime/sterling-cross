Privacy Policy

Last updated January 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, intake application, or consultation booking, we collect the information you provide directly, including your name, contact details, and any details you share about your legal matter. If you upload photos or documents through our intake application, we collect those files as well.

How We Use It

Information you submit is used solely to evaluate your inquiry, schedule consultations, and, if you become a client, to represent you. Uploaded photos and documents are reviewed by your attorney and staff working on your matter and are not used for any other purpose. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Third-Party Form Processing

Our intake application, including any files you upload, is processed through Basin (usebasin.com), a third-party form backend provider. Our general contact form is processed through Web3Forms (web3forms.com). Both providers receive only the information you submit through the relevant form and are contractually limited from using it for their own purposes. See each provider's privacy policy for details on how they handle data in transit.

Payment Information

We do not collect card or payment information anywhere on this site. Legal Plan billing is arranged directly with our team by phone or email after you submit an application, and consultations booked through our Contact page are free. If online payment processing is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to name the payment processor used.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share with us, consistent with applicable United States data protection standards.

Contacting Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@sterlingcross.law or by using our Contact page.
