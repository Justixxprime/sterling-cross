Common questions

Is this consultation free?
Yes, this initial consultation is free and carries no obligation. If you decide afterward that you'd like ongoing coverage, we'll walk you through applying for a Legal Plan.
What's the difference between this and a Legal Plan?
This form books a one-time, no-cost conversation with an attorney. A Legal Plan is an ongoing membership, unlimited advice calls, document review, and discounted representation for a flat monthly fee. Many people book a consultation first, then apply for a plan once they see what's covered.
Which areas of law do you handle?
25 practice areas in all, from corporate and capital markets to family law and estate planning. If your matter falls outside these, we will point you to someone who can help.
How quickly will someone respond?
Within one business day for all calls and emails, and often sooner during business hours.
Can I reach an office outside the US?
Yes, all 14 offices can be reached through this form, just note your preferred office and time zone in your message.
Is this form secure and confidential?
Yes, but submitting this form does not create an attorney-client relationship or privilege until we have formally engaged.
