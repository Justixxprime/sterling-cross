How this firm actually started.

Sterling and Cross opened in Chicago in 2012 with two attorneys and a single shared office. There was no grand plan beyond doing right by every client who walked through the door, no matter how small or complicated their matter seemed. The first clients were referrals from friends and family, the kind of relationships where a bad outcome would have been personal, not just professional.

That pressure never really went away, even as the firm grew. Every office we opened, starting with London in 2016, was staffed by people who trained under that same instinct: understand the situation fully before recommending anything, and never treat a client's problem as routine just because we've seen something similar before.

More than a decade later, we have grown to over 1,200 attorneys across 25 practice areas and 14 offices on six continents. The firm looks nothing like it did in 2012, but the way we actually work with a client, on the phone, in a negotiation, at 11pm before a filing deadline, has not changed. That same instinct is why we built Sterling & Cross Legal Plans, a membership that puts an attorney on call for the everyday legal questions that never needed a full-blown engagement in the first place.
