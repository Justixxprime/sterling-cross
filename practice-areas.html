Wrongful Death

Every state defines a wrongful death claim a little differently, but most require proving the same four things: the defendant owed the victim a duty of care, the defendant breached that duty, the breach caused the death, and the death produced compensable losses for surviving family members. We handle these claims in the state where the death occurred, applying that state's specific elements, damages rules, and filing deadlines.

What We Handle

Fatal Motor Vehicle Accidents

Claims arising from fatal car, truck, and motorcycle crashes.

Fatal Workplace Accidents

Medical Malpractice Deaths

Fatalities caused by a provider's departure from the standard of care.

Defective Product Deaths

Fatalities caused by dangerous or defective products.

Survival Actions

Claims for what the deceased could have recovered had they survived.

Economic & Non-Economic Damages

Lost income, funeral costs, loss of companionship, and more.

Who We Help

Surviving spouses, children, and next of kin Estates pursuing a claim on behalf of the deceased Families across all fifty states
