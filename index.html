About Us

One firm, twelve years, six continents.

Founded in Chicago in 2012, Sterling & Cross has grown from a single practice into a firm with 14 offices spanning the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia, and Australia. Every office operates as one firm, not a federation of local practices, so a client in São Paulo gets the same standard of work as a client in Singapore.

Our Process

Designed to simplify complex matters, at every stage.

Whether it's a first consultation or the fifth year of an ongoing relationship, every client moves through the same disciplined process.

01

Discovery & Consultation

We start by listening. A confidential conversation to understand your matter, your goals, and what a good outcome actually looks like.

02

Strategy Development

Your attorney team builds a strategy against real timelines and budget, not a generic playbook, before any work begins.

03

Execution & Support

Negotiation, filings, or courtroom advocacy, carried out by the same team that scoped the strategy, with regular direct updates.

04

Ongoing Partnership

Most matters aren't a one-time event. We stay close after resolution, so the next issue gets ahead of you, not the other way around.
