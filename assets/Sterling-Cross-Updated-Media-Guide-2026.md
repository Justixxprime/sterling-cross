# Sterling & Cross — Updated Media Guide 2026
**World-Class Visual Identity | Free-Source Priority Edition**

> Goal: Make the site read like a top-tier global firm (Kirkland, Latham, Clifford Chance, Wachtell level) even before the firm is that size.  
> Method: Real named landmarks, architecture over people, restraint over polish, absolute consistency across attorney headshots, and zero generic “law firm stock” tropes.

This document updates and consolidates:
- `Sterling-Cross-Elite-Photo-Spec.md`
- `Sterling-Cross-Photography-Video-Guide.md`
- `Sterling-Cross-Questionnaire-and-Media-Guide_1.md`

It adds practical free commercial-use sources, exact search strings, priority order, naming conventions, and a licensing checklist so you can finish the remaining images quickly and at elite quality.

---

## 1. Core Visual Rules (Never Break These)

1. **Architecture > People** whenever possible. Landmark buildings, conference tables, server corridors, courthouse columns, empty stadium bowls, etc.
2. **Named landmarks only.** “Chicago skyline with Willis Tower” beats “a modern city skyline.” Recognizability in under one second.
3. **Restraint.** Shallow depth of field, directional window light, dark/neutral + gold accents, no big grins, no gavels, no scales of justice, no smiling groups in blue suits.
4. **Consistency is more important than individual perfection.** All 16 attorney headshots must share the exact same backdrop, lighting, crop, and expression language.
5. **No identifiable real people as “your attorneys”** unless they are actually your attorneys (or you have model releases + consistent styling).

## 2. Free Commercial-Use Photo Sources (Ranked for This Project)

| Priority | Site | Best For | License | Notes |
|----------|------|----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | **Unsplash** (unsplash.com) | Editorial architecture, real skylines, dramatic interiors, abstract tech | Unsplash License – commercial OK, no attribution required | Highest “pro photographer” quality. Start here. |
| 2 | **Pexels** (pexels.com) | Clean office interiors, professional conversations, tech/cyber, energy, soft lifestyle | Pexels License – commercial OK, no attribution | Excellent lighting & modern corporate feel. Also strong free video. |
| 3 | **Pixabay** (pixabay.com) | Skylines, architecture, abstract graphics, vectors for decorative elements | Pixabay Content License – commercial OK | Huge library + free vectors/illustrations. |
| 4 | **Adobe Stock Free Collection** (stock.adobe.com → Free filter) | Highest production-value corporate & architecture | Adobe free licence – commercial OK | Closest to paid quality while free. |
| 5 | **StockSnap.io** | Clean architecture & landscapes | CC0 | Pure public-domain style. |
| 6 | **Vecteezy** (free section) | Photos + matching vectors/illustrations | Check free asset licence | Good for decorative graphics that still feel premium. |
| 7 | **Burst by Shopify** | Straightforward business interiors | Free commercial | Clean and business-friendly. |
| 8 | **Negative Space / Reshot / SplitShire / Life of Pix** | More unique editorial shots | Usually CC0 or free commercial | Smaller libraries → less overused images. |

**Always** open the individual image page and confirm the licence before downloading. Prefer originals ≥ 2000–4000 px on the long side, then crop to the exact sizes listed in the Elite Spec.

- Antitrust / Competition → `modern glass office building exterior dramatic`
- Banking & Finance → `trading floor motion blur terminal`
- Capital Markets / IPO → `stock exchange floor energy` or `opening bell style`
- Corporate M&A → `signing table pens folio warm light`
- Litigation → `empty grand courtroom rotunda` or `federal courthouse columns dawn`
- Private Equity → `closing table signature page desk lamp`
- Restructuring → `building facade hard shadow clinical light`
- Technology → `server room blue LED corridor shallow focus`
- Data Privacy & Cybersecurity → `dark server racks green blue lights`
- Energy & Infrastructure → `wind farm golden hour` or `transmission towers`
- Environmental & ESG → `green building facade next to older architecture`
- Insurance → `clean corporate lobby minimal`
- Immigration → `passport boarding pass elegant still life`
- Sports & Entertainment → `empty stadium upper bowl dusk` or `soundstage rigging`
- Personal Injury → `empty crosswalk dusk restrained` (never graphic accident imagery)
- Wrongful Death → `single chair soft window light empty room` (most restrained image on the entire site)
- Government Benefits → `federal building public entrance dignified`
- Probate → `courthouse records room warm desk light ledger`

### Industries (Tall Cards)
- Private Capital → `glass-walled investment conference room skyline behind`
- Real Estate → `landmark tower looking straight up dramatic vertical`
- Industrials → `factory floor robotics leading lines`
- Media & Telecom → `broadcast control room monitors` or `broadcast tower`
- Consumer & Retail → `flagship retail interior architecture only`
- Transportation → `modern airport terminal or high-speed rail station architecture`

### Locations / Offices
Search for the actual city + “prestige office building exterior” or “modern law office lobby interior”. Prefer real landmark buildings that could plausibly house a top firm.

### Attorney Headshots
If using stock temporarily: search `professional headshot neutral backdrop soft light` then force consistency in post (same crop, same grade, same eye line).  
Best long-term: one real session with identical setup for every attorney.

### Decorative / Small Graphics
- Abstract geometric dark
- Marble texture close-up
- Architectural cornice detail
- Subtle data visualization dark mode
- Single-color line-art scales or architectural motif (or create in Figma / Illustrator)

## 5. Technical Specs (Quick Reference)

| Category | Typical Size / Crop | Format | Notes |
|----------|---------------------|--------|-------|
| Homepage / section heroes | Full-width, usually 1920×800–1080 or larger | JPG (quality 80–85) or WebP | High impact, architecture preferred |
| Practice / industry cards | Landscape or tall as designed | JPG | Consistent aspect ratio across the grid |
| Attorney main bio | 500 × 667 (3:4) | JPG | Same for every attorney |
| Attorney directory / sidebar | 400 × 540 (3:4) | JPG | Slightly tighter crop of the same original |
| Small avatars | 80 × 80 | JPG / PNG | Neutral dark backdrop |
| Decorative graphics | As needed (often square or wide) | PNG with transparency preferred for overlays | Can be pure graphic, not photo |
| Location cards | ~500 × 380 landscape | JPG | One exterior (or strong interior) per city |

Always optimize for web (compress without visible quality loss). Keep original high-res masters in a separate `/assets/originals/` folder if possible.

## 6. File Naming Convention (Keep It Clean)

Use the same style already present in the repo:

- `about-hero.jpg`
- `practice-areas-hero.jpg`
- `Corporate-M&A.jpg`
- `Technology.jpg`
- `global-skyline.jpg`
- `attorney-jane-smith.jpg` (or `-headshot.jpg` / `-card.jpg` if you need two crops)
- `office-london-exterior.jpg`
- `case-study-cross-border-merger.jpg`

Lowercase or Title-Case is fine as long as it is consistent and matches how the HTML references the files.

## 7. Licensing & Usage Checklist (Do This Every Time)

- [ ] Confirmed the image is free for commercial use on a client website
- [ ] No requirement to give attribution (or attribution is optional and you decide)
- [ ] Image does not contain identifiable people who could be mistaken for firm attorneys or clients (unless released)
- [ ] No logos, trademarks, or branded clothing visible that could cause issues
- [ ] Cropped and graded to match the rest of the site’s visual language
- [ ] Optimized for web and correctly named
- [ ] Original high-res saved separately if you might need it later

---

## 8. When to Spend Real Budget

Free sources get you 80–90 % of the way to a world-class look. Spend real money only on:

1. **One consistent attorney headshot session** for the entire team (highest trust impact).
2. The single strongest “proof-of-scale” case-study or notable-matter image.
3. Custom video (firm overview film, practice-area films) if you want motion.
4. Bespoke illustrations or map graphics that need to feel completely proprietary.

Everything else can (and should) come from the free sources above while you grow.

---

## 9. Quick Start Workflow

1. Open the Elite Photo Spec and identify the next 5–10 highest-priority missing or weak images.
2. Copy the exact search string from Section 4 into Unsplash → Pexels → Pixabay in that order.
3. Download 2–3 candidates per slot.
4. Crop to the required aspect ratio, apply a consistent grade (slightly cool or neutral, high contrast, subtle gold accent if needed).
5. Name correctly, drop into `/assets/`, update the HTML `src` if the filename changed.
6. Test on the live page at desktop + mobile.
7. Repeat.
