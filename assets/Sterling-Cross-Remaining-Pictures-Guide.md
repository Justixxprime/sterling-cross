| Rank | Website | Why It Is Best for Sterling & Cross | Licence | Best Use |

| **1** | **[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)** | Highest “pro photographer” quality. Best real landmarks, architecture, dramatic skylines, and restrained editorial shots. **How to use them:**  
Start on **Unsplash** → if you cannot find the exact feel, go to **Pexels** → use **Pixabay** for vectors or extra architecture options.  
Always open the individual image page and confirm the licence before downloading. Prefer originals 2000 px+ on the long side, then crop to the sizes in this guide.
